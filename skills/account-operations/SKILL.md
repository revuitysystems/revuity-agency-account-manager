# Account Operations

Treat each account as documented promises, deliverables, decisions, risks, approvals, commercial boundaries, and outcomes.

## Source discipline
Use signed scope/contract documents, approved proposals/SOWs, project plans, client communications, task systems, meeting records, and approved commercial data. Do not treat a suggestion, draft, meeting comment, or informal message as an approved scope or commercial change.

## Meeting prep
Produce account objective, deliverables, open client/internal commitments, decisions, approvals, risks, scope questions, outcome evidence, and recommended agenda.

## Meeting recap
Separate confirmed decisions, client actions, internal actions, deadlines, approvals, approved scope changes, potential/unapproved scope changes, risks, and next checkpoint. Always preserve proposed vs. approved.

## Deliverable tracking
Capture source scope/reference, owner, status, dependency, due date, client input, approval state, next action, and completion condition.

## Scope protection
Flag work potentially outside documented scope. Prepare a neutral change brief with request, source scope, gap, delivery impact, timing impact, and commercial decision required. Do not alter terms automatically.

## Client health / risk review
Use evidence such as missed commitments, unresolved issues, approval delays, delivery defects, scope friction, stakeholder changes, outcome progress, AR issues when supplied, and renewal timing. Separate observed facts from interpretation.

## Renewal preparation
Summarize outcomes delivered, evidence, unresolved risks, open commitments, scope evolution, expansion needs, decision timeline, stakeholders, and conversation plan. Do not invent ROI or outcome claims.

## Authority
May organize, analyze, draft, and recommend. Explicit human authorization is required for consequential external sends, scope/SOW changes, discounts/credits/pricing/payment terms, contract amendments, invoice changes, promises of unapproved dates/resources, and acceptance of client legal/security terms.
